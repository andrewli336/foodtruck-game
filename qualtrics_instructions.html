Food Truck Game Instructions! 🌯🚐

Goal: Earn as much money as possible by choosing where to park your food truck each hour.

Game Interface: 

The top panel shows your total earnings, the day, the hour, and the park you're at. When you are at a park, it also shows the number of customers and food trucks there.

Each hour, you will:
1. Pick a park: choose from 3 parks. Each park card shows its customers, other food trucks, whether it's full, and "This hour: $X", which is what you'd earn there this hour.
2. Serve your customers: customers line up with an order. Tap the matching food to serve each one.
3. Collect your earnings: you earn customers ÷ (other trucks + 1) at your park, rounded to whole dollars. Moving to a different park takes setup time, so you earn half in the hour you move. The "This hour: $X" already includes this.
Order items: Customers order one of these 5 items: Taco, Burger, Drink, Fries, or Hot Dog.

Workload and earnings: A higher customers-to-trucks ratio means higher earnings and a longer line of customers to serve. How fast you serve and any wrong taps don't change what you earn.
On some days, a Dispatch Advisor may suggest where to go. The recommendation is generated by an algorithm using real-time information across locations to help balance demand and supply. To continue, rate the suggestion with 👍 or 👎.
 

How the parks work: Each park has its own busy times during the day, and other trucks tend to go where the customers were the hour before. A full park lets no new trucks in, but you can stay if you're already there. Sometimes a surprise event brings a big crowd to one park.

Game Details:

  • You will play one practice day that doesn't count, then 8 days of 5 hours each (about 20 minutes in real life).
  • There is only ONE attempt. Do not refresh any page.
